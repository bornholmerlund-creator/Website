## The situation

Meridian Distribution had a proposal on the desk for a new ERP, driven by genuine pain: reports took days, stock figures were unreliable, and finance lived in spreadsheets. The assumption — encouraged by the vendor pitching the replacement — was that the current system had to go.

## What we did

We ran a two-week, vendor-neutral health check before any contract was signed.

- **Assessed configuration, data quality, integration and adoption** against how the business actually needed to operate.
- **Quantified every gap in hours, error rates and cash**, so the board could decide on evidence rather than vendor narrative.
- **Found the system was fundamentally capable** — but unintegrated, partly unconfigured, and barely adopted beyond its basics.

We then delivered a 90-day optimisation: integrating the warehouse system, switching on and configuring unused modules, cleaning master data, and building governed reporting on top.

## The outcome

Meridian kept its existing ERP and captured most of the benefit the replacement had promised, for a fraction of the cost and disruption. The finance team recovered around nine hours a week previously lost to manual reconciliation, and stock figures became reliable enough to plan against.

> "We were three signatures away from spending one-point-four million pounds we didn't need to. The honest answer cost us a fraction of that." — Managing Director, Meridian Distribution
