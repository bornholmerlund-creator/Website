## The situation

Northcare Services ran multiple care sites on systems that had grown independently. The same client, service and staff records existed differently across them. Board meetings routinely stalled on which version of a number was correct, and a planned analytics initiative had quietly stalled on data that wasn't fit to use.

## What we did

We treated the symptom — distrusted reports — as a governance and architecture problem, not a dashboard problem.

- **Baselined master-data quality**, measuring completeness, duplication and accuracy across the estate.
- **Assigned clear data owners and stewards** with realistic, documented responsibilities.
- **Agreed canonical definitions** for the metrics the board relied on, ending the definitional arguments.
- **Applied validation at the point of entry** so quality was preserved, not just restored once.
- **Built governed reporting** on the cleaned foundation.

## The outcome

Master-data accuracy rose from 71% to 96% and held, because ownership and entry-time validation kept it there. Board reporting that once took days of manual assembly — and still got challenged — became a single, trusted set of numbers produced in minutes. The stalled analytics programme restarted on a foundation it could finally rely on.

> "We stopped arguing about whose number was right and started making decisions. That shift is worth more than the dashboards." — Operations Director, Northcare Services
