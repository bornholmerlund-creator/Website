## The situation

Precision Components Group ran a discrete-manufacturing operation on an ERP that the vendor had announced end-of-life. Twelve years of part numbers, BOMs, supplier records and open transactions lived in a system nobody fully understood, with critical logic in spreadsheets maintained by two long-serving staff.

The board had approved a replacement. What they had not approved was a repeat of the failed project three years earlier, which slipped twice and went live with ledgers that took a quarter to reconcile.

## What we did

We were brought in alongside the chosen platform partner with one mandate: make the data and the cutover trustworthy.

- **Profiled the legacy data in week one**, surfacing duplicate parts, orphaned BOM lines and inconsistent units of measure before they could derail the timeline.
- **Documented every mapping and transformation decision**, so nothing was improvised under pressure.
- **Ran four full dry runs**, each timed and reconciled, until cutover was a predictable, rehearsed sequence rather than a gamble.
- **Integrated the WMS and the e-commerce channel** so there was no re-keying from the first day live.

## The outcome

The go-live hit its original date. The reconciliation pack tied out record counts and financial balances from source to target before the operations team logged in on Monday morning. The first month-end close completed in eleven days — faster than the legacy system had managed in years.
