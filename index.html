About us

Complex data, made simple.

Our vision is to make complex data simple, so small and medium-sized businesses can run on the same structure and automation as large enterprises, without the enterprise overhead.

We kept meeting capable teams who spent their days copying numbers between systems, fixing the same errors every month and waiting for reports nobody fully trusted. The problem was rarely the people, and rarely the software. It was the structure underneath.

So that is where we start. We put structure into your data, automate the routine work around it and make sure your ERP supports the way you actually operate. When the foundation is right, efficiency follows.

How a project runs

Most projects take 4–10 weeks from first meeting to hand-over.

  1. Step 1 1–2 weeks

    Map

    We review your systems, data and manual routines, and hand you a prioritised list of where time is lost.

  2. Step 2 2–8 weeks

    Build

    We fix the structure and set up automations in small steps, testing each one with the people who use it.

  3. Step 3 + 30-day check-in

    Hand over

    Documentation and training for your team, plus a check-in after 30 days to make sure everything holds.
